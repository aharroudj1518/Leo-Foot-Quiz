Something is not working

Tell us whether you are using Android, iPhone or a web browser, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Include the app version if available. A screenshot can help; remove personal information first.

Your quiz progress is stored on your device. Before clearing app data or reinstalling, consider Settings → Export my progress. An export is a readable copy for your records; the current app does not provide an import button. We cannot recover local progress from a Leoqo cloud account.

Included questions and saved Matchday briefings work offline. Source websites and purchase verification need a connection. Dated briefings may be marked as archive; that is not an error.

Report an incorrect or unclear question

Use the question's report button to save a local report. Reports are not sent automatically. To send one to us, open Settings → Export question reports and attach the relevant report to an email.

You can also email the question wording or identifier, the edition/year shown, your suggested correction and a reliable source link. This helps distinguish an error from a question about a historical season. There is no need to include your full progress export.

Legends Pack purchases and restoration

The Legends Pack is offered only in store builds where purchases are enabled. The browser version and some test builds do not offer purchases. The shop shows availability and the store's price before you confirm.

  1. Check that you are signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account used for the purchase.
  2. Open Settings → Purchases & restore, complete the adult step and choose Restore purchases.
  3. If payment is still pending, wait for the store to confirm it and try restoration. Do not buy again simply because access has not appeared.

If you still need help, email the platform, approximate purchase date, product name and the error shown. For a completed purchase, the store order/transaction reference may help us locate it. Do not send a password or full card number.

Include the Support ID shown in Settings → Purchases & restore, if available. Complete the adult step, then select the ID or use Share my support ID. It helps us locate the associated RevenueCat customer record. You do not need to make a purchase to obtain help or an ID in an enabled store build. An ID is not available in browser play, Expo Go or builds with the shop disabled. If none is shown, email the details you have.

Payment and refund decisions are handled through the store's process: Google Play refund help or Apple's Report a Problem. Deleting quiz progress does not cancel or refund a purchase.

Your information and deletion requests

Clear progress from this device

Choose Settings → Privacy & about Leoqo → Delete my local progress. This resets saved rounds, daily records, settings and local question reports. It does not remove cached briefings, files you exported, copies you shared, device backups, or records held by purchase services.

To remove other local app/site storage, use your device or browser's storage controls. Delete exports and copies elsewhere using the controls for the service holding them. Clearing all app data can also remove local purchase-service caches; use the store's restore feature if needed.

Ask about information held outside your device

Email info@novaspheretechnology.co.uk with the subject Leoqo privacy request. Tell us whether your request concerns a support email, a website visit, or a purchase, and whether you want access, correction or deletion. For support emails, use the original email address where possible. We may ask for limited information to identify the relevant record.

A purchase-service record can exist even if you never bought the pack. If available, include your Support ID from Settings → Purchases & restore after the adult step. Opening the enabled shop starts purchase-service processing and the SDK can remain configured after you leave. If you already have an ID saved, you can send it directly. Do not post your ID publicly or send someone else's ID.

Email support in the app's shop or privacy screen may include the ID in an email draft when available. Check the draft before sending; opening it does not send it automatically.

There is no Leoqo login account to close. Local reset does not erase RevenueCat or store records, and the app has no remote deletion button. We will explain any provider-specific steps or retention that applies to your request. If your concern relates to a child, a parent or carer can contact us without attaching the child's full quiz history.

See the privacy policy for the data handled by local play, optional online features and our Vercel-hosted website.
