Playing and saving progress

You can play without a Leoqo account. The quiz does not ask for your name, email address, birthday or location. There are no advertisements, and this version does not include a general-purpose advertising or analytics integration.

Your device stores answers, scores, questions seen, mistakes, hint use, an unfinished round, recent completed rounds, daily-completion dates and settings. Saved rounds can include their questions so that you can still review them after content changes. A question report stores its question identifier, selected reason and date.

We use this local information to resume play, show results, calculate streaks and help you practise. It is stored in the mobile app's local storage or, for browser play, your browser's local storage. Ordinary quiz play does not upload your progress or reports. The app keeps up to 100 completed rounds; separate daily-completion records can remain longer. Local storage is not an encryption guarantee and may be included in device or browser backups.

Football briefings

Included and saved briefings can be played offline. They show their edition dates; older editions are labelled as archive. This does not require a live news service.

Where Check for new editions is available, choosing it after the adult step makes an HTTPS request to the content host. There is no automatic news polling in the current app. The host receives connection information such as your IP address and request details. Your quiz answers, progress and local reports are not sent with that request. Downloaded editions are saved on your device.

Shop and purchases

Where purchases are enabled in an Android or iPhone store build, Leoqo uses RevenueCat and Google Play or Apple's App Store to show products, verify a purchase and restore access to the Legends Pack. Browser play does not offer purchases.

Opening the enabled shop after the adult step starts purchase-service processing before you buy anything. The purchase SDK can remain configured after you leave the shop; completing or leaving the adult step does not switch it off. Leoqo does not provide a personal account ID. RevenueCat's SDK creates and stores a random app-user identifier instead. It can distinguish a purchase customer, so it does not mean that all purchase data is anonymous.

RevenueCat and the store process purchase history, transaction information, receipts or purchase tokens, product and entitlement status, and relevant app/device information. These support purchasing and restoration. RevenueCat also processes purchase and entitlement information for purchase-related analytics. Leoqo does not send your quiz answers or question reports to RevenueCat. The store handles your payment credentials; Leoqo does not receive your card number.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy. Purchase records held by these services are separate from your local quiz progress.

Website hosting and connection information

The website, including these support and privacy pages, is hosted by Vercel. Visiting it sends technical connection information to Vercel, such as your IP address, requested page, request time and browser information. Vercel may derive an approximate location from the IP address. It processes hosting information to deliver the site, maintain the service and protect against abuse.

Local quiz storage does not remove these hosting requests. This site does not add an advertising or visitor-analytics script. Vercel's platform processing is described in its Privacy Notice. Retention depends on the service and its configuration; we do not promise a fixed deletion period for provider records. Hosting and purchase providers may process information outside your country, as described in their policies.

Sharing, exports and source links

Sharing is optional. A shared score contains the round label/date where applicable, score and hint/miss indicators, rather than the correct answers. A progress export contains saved answers, including typed responses, settings and round records. A reports export includes reasons and dates.

The app opens the device share interface or creates a download. Browser score sharing can also copy the result to your clipboard. Another service receives the information when you choose to send it there. Keep only the exports you need, and check their contents before sharing.

Source links open external websites after the adult step. Those sites receive their own connection information and follow their own privacy practices. We do not control information you subsequently give them.

Contacting support

If you email us, the support mailbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Email support opened from the app's shop or privacy screen may prefill your Support ID in an email draft when available; you can review it before sending. Opening a draft does not send it automatically. We use the information you send to respond, investigate the issue and handle related follow-up. Correspondence remains in the support mailbox unless deleted; you can ask us to review or delete it.

Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary personal information. Children should ask a parent or carer to contact us. The support page explains what details help with a question report or purchase problem.

Children and families

Leoqo is designed for football fans aged 10 and above, including families. Free quiz progress and question reports are stored locally as described above. In the app, purchases, normal sharing/export actions and external links use an adult confirmation step.

The displayed challenge and checkbox are a feature safeguard. They do not verify a person's age or parental identity and are not a verified parental-consent process. A parent or carer should supervise online and purchase features. If you are concerned about information relating to a child, contact us using the privacy email above.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can export local information from Settings → Export my progress or Export question reports. To reset it in the app, choose Settings → Privacy & about Leoqo → Delete my local progress. This clears the saved rounds, daily records, settings and local reports used by the app.

Local reset does not delete cached briefings, exported or shared copies, RevenueCat information, or app-store purchase records. It does not cancel or refund a purchase. Device or browser controls can clear app/site storage. Copies in backups or other services have their own deletion controls; resetting the app is not a secure erase of every copy.

For information held outside your device, email info@novaspheretechnology.co.uk and describe your request. You may request access, correction or deletion of information relating to you. We may need limited details to locate a record or verify that a request concerns you. We will explain if a request needs to go to a provider or if records must be retained, for example for a transaction dispute or legal obligation. Do not send card details.

For purchase-service information, include your Support ID if available. In an enabled native store build, open Settings → Purchases & restore, complete the adult step, then select the ID or use Share my support ID. You do not need to buy anything. The ID helps us locate the associated RevenueCat customer record; opening the enabled shop still starts the processing described above. Share it only with support or a service handling your request.

There is no Leoqo login account to close. The current app has no remote deletion button. The privacy support instructions distinguish local reset from provider-held records.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its review date when the app's data handling changes. Contact the Leoqo Football Quiz support address above with questions about this policy.
