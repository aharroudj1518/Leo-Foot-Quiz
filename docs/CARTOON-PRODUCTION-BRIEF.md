# Player cartoon production brief

## Purpose
Create a coherent illustrated player-recognition collection for Leoqo Football Quiz on Android, iOS and web. Photographs remain an optional parallel clue style. This commission must produce recognisable individual footballers, not generic mascots or one reused face. The app’s existing lion emblem sets the surrounding brand palette, not the facial drawing style.

## Pilot and full scope
Quote separately for a three-player pilot (Lionel Messi, Aitana Bonmatí and Ronaldinho) and the full 50-player roster in CARTOON-ROSTER.csv. Do not start the full set until the pilot is approved. The pilot must show that current players, women and former players all fit one illustration system. Quote revisions, delivery schedule and any continuing licence fees separately. No budget or order is committed by this brief.

## Art direction
Original, warm sports caricature with expressive faces, clear silhouettes, crisp ink contours and restrained shaded colour. Head and upper torso, slightly exaggerated individual features, consistent camera angle and lighting. Preserve distinctions in face shape, age, skin tone, hairstyle and facial hair without demeaning exaggeration. Do not trace a competitor screenshot or imitate its exact drawing. No player names, initials, numbers, signatures, club crests, sponsors, branded kit patterns or watermarks within the puzzle art. Plain unbranded tops; identity should come from the face.

## Technical delivery
- Transparent PNG, sRGB, 1024 × 1280 pixels, one player per file.
- Face and hair inside the central 80% of the width; leave 10% clear margins and room above hair.
- Consistent head size across the set; recognisable at a rendered width of 100 pixels and clear at 240 pixels.
- Editable vector or layered master when available, with fonts converted to paths if any incidental text is agreed.
- Exact filenames from the roster, lowercase ASCII IDs. No packed contact sheets as the only deliverable.
- Per-file creator, creation date, reference sources, permitted uses, required credit, licence evidence, territories and expiry/renewal conditions.

## Uses to quote explicitly
Embedding and offline distribution inside free apps with advertising and in-app purchases, iOS/Android store screenshots, the web app and app marketing. State whether resizing, layout cropping, background placement and animation are permitted. Identify any exclusions or approvals needed. The artist’s copyright permission and footballer name/image/likeness clearance are separate questions; do not claim the former automatically supplies the latter. Final release assessment is still required.

## Acceptance
Review the pilot in the real question frame and album at phone size, with names hidden. Check recognition, consistent style, no answer-revealing labels, no copied marks, export quality and complete provenance. One approved pilot establishes the art direction before the remaining roster is commissioned. A later independent release reviewer records final clearance against the delivered files; do not fill approval fields from this brief.
