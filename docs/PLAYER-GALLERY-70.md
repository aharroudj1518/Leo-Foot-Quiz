# Player gallery expansion — 11 September 2026

Twenty new source photographs expand the playable gallery from 50 to 70 players: 34 World stars, 18 Game changers and 18 The greats. The full bank now contains 1,822 questions, including 82 visual questions (70 portraits, six badge puzzles and six stadium clues).

The additions include Benzema, Kroos, Neuer, Courtois, Alisson, Pedri, Musiala, Yamal, Lauren James, Chloe Kelly, Alessia Russo, Salma Paralluelo, Linda Caicedo, Caroline Graham Hansen, Pirlo, Totti, Puyol, Ibrahimović, Rivaldo and Roberto Carlos. Full names and recorded aliases work with the existing answer checker; album progress remains keyed by stable question IDs.

Every new file has an identity source, Commons source, creator, licence URL and SHA-256 record. All twenty files were visually inspected for face visibility and answer-revealing captions. Rivaldo uses a low-resolution archive photograph with his face near the bottom; the full frame is retained with contain sizing. The other existing images were not replaced. Source files remain unchanged.

The importer now accepts the exact CC BY 3.0 Brazil variant after checking its official deed: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/br/deed.en. Attribution and licence links are available in Artwork credits. This licence check and visual inspection do not constitute likeness, trademark or commercial-release approval. The asset register remains review-required and commerce stays disabled.

Validation: TypeScript, all 96 unit tests, web export and 16 desktop/phone browser tests passed. Those browser tests load all 70 album portraits, complete a ten-question photo round, restore progress, practise mistakes, and check narrow-screen navigation. Two additional phone checks cover full-frame rendering for portrait and landscape source images; their rendered screenshots were inspected. These are browser checks, not physical-device acceptance.

The visual-content generator now replaces questions in place and appends new IDs, avoiding unrelated catalogue reordering. This batch does not add cartoons or complete the larger commercial-quality goal.
