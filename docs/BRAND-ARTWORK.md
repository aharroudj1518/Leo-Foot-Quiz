# Illustrated emblem

The lion-and-football emblem replaces the template football-outline header mark
and Expo launcher/favicon artwork. The local preview uses the new asset;
previously downloaded native binaries retain their old launcher icon until rebuilt.

Asset: `assets/brand/lion-ball.png`. Generated with the built-in image-generation
tool on 9 September 2026, with no reference image. Original generated file:
`exec-0ab81e7c-7d9a-4151-b96b-cab1f70a2362.png`.

Final prompt:

> Create one polished mobile football trivia app icon, square 1024x1024. Original bold illustrated emblem: a clever golden lion face, its mane cleverly forming the silhouette of a football, with small ivory football-panel shapes integrated into the mane. Expressive confident eyes, memorable asymmetric forelock, strong silhouette readable at 48 pixels. Sports editorial illustration with crisp ink contours, subtle print grain, dimensional cut-paper shading, beautifully controlled detail. Deep midnight teal full-bleed background, warm golden ochre and ivory emblem centered with generous 16 percent safe margins. No letters, no words, no shields, no crown, no existing club symbols, no watermark. Designed as the identity of Leo Foot Quiz, a characterful football knowledge game. Single finished icon, not a mockup or presentation sheet.

The resulting square raster is 1254 pixels wide. Expo generates platform icon
sizes during export/build. Android currently uses the conventional launcher icon;
a separately composed adaptive foreground and monochrome asset remain future work.

This brand asset does not fulfill the separate player-cartoon requirement. The
current five player cards still use attributed photographs. A consistent cartoon
player collection remains required, with provenance recorded for every image.
