# Rules content: first source check

9 September 2026. Checked the answers in all 15 rules questions against IFAB's current 2026/27 online laws. This is an assistant source check, not independent editorial approval; no approval ledger entries were created. The build uploads preceded this report and contain the existing wording.

| Questions | Source | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rule-1, rule-2 | [Law 3.1](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players/) | Eleven is the maximum. Seven is the general minimum, but the current law identifies exceptions involving temporary absences and protocols; advantage can also continue after deliberate departures. Clarify rule-1 as asking for the maximum, and scope rule-2 to starting a standard match rather than making an absolute claim about continued play. |
| rule-3, rule-4 | [Law 7.1–7.2](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-duration-of-the-match/) | 45-minute halves and normally at most 15 minutes at half-time are supported, with the law's competition/agreement qualifications. Existing wording is qualified. |
| rule-5, rule-6 | [Law 12.4](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/fouls-and-misconduct/) | Red signals sending-off and yellow a caution. |
| rule-7, rule-8 | [Law 15](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-throw-in/) | No goal directly from a throw-in; delivery uses both hands from behind and over the head. |
| rule-9 | [Law 14.1](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-penalty-kick/) | Penalty mark answer is supported. Current detailed placement requires part of the ball touching or overhanging its centre; avoid expanding the simple answer into an inaccurate placement rule. |
| rule-10 | [Law 1.6](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-field-of-play/) | The penalty mark is 11 metres from the midpoint between the posts. |
| rule-11 | [Law 8 introduction](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-start-and-restart-of-play/) | Kick-off starts both halves. |
| rule-12, rule-13 | [Law 11.3](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/offside/) | Direct receipt from a goal kick or throw-in has no offside offence. |
| rule-14 | [Law 9.1](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-ball-in-and-out-of-play/) | Whole ball crossing the boundary is correct for the scoped boundary-line question. Other out-of-play cases exist. |
| rule-15 | [Law 5.1](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-referee/) | Referee enforcement authority is supported. |
