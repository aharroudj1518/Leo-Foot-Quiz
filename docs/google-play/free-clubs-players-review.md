- `player-17`: retain the selected-clubs qualifier, but explain the chronology precisely: “Sam Kerr has represented Australia internationally. These are selected clubs in the order she first joined them; she also alternated Australian and US seasons. This is not a current-club claim.” Perth was her first senior club and later also a seasonal return; the arrows must not imply uninterrupted exclusive contracts.
- Replace generic, redirected, inaccessible or insufficient source links with the primary sources in the tables. “Keep” rows need no source change. Preserve Salah’s permanent-clubs qualifier: his Fiorentina spell was a loan and Roma initially loaned him before a permanent move.
