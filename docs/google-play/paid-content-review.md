# Paid question review — 6 October 2026

The 40 paid answers, their competition/year wording, country hints and rendered answer choices passed a separate factual review against UEFA's own records. All **65 UEFA final questions** now use individually checked sources and an explicit generator mapping. Compared with the original uploaded ZIP, **17 free source links changed**; the 40 paid links and eight older free links were already correct and are preserved in the final result. This review changed no winner, difficulty label, answer option, price, entitlement or commerce flag.

This is a scoped evidence record for the paid pack, with additional verification of the 25 free Champions League winners. It does not approve the remaining 95 questions in the 160-question bank, the news feed, or native billing. `independentEditorialApproval` remains `false`; it was not changed or bypassed by this work.

## Method and scope

A reviewer separate from the question producer inspected all 40 paid questions. A second reviewing agent independently checked the 1976–1995 primary pages. Verification compared the question's final year and answer with the actual UEFA season heading and winner section, rather than treating a successful HTTP response or a year in a URL as proof. The same check was then extended to all 25 `clubs-2000`–`clubs-2024` questions because the route convention changes within that series.

The primary pages for 2022–2024 repeatedly timed out on direct open. Their indexed UEFA page bodies still exposed the season headings, winner sections and final-date image captions; these were used for those three rows. The other 62 season pages were directly opened. This limitation is specific to access during the review and does not imply their links are broken for app users.

Country hints were checked for consistency with the winning clubs. The historical Yugoslavia reference for 1991 is appropriate; its present-tense phrasing could be polished. Germany in the Bayern/Hamburg hints is geographically correct, although “West Germany at the time” would make the historical context more precise. The 1974 trophy was decided after a replay; Bayern remains the correct winning club. UEFA's [1973/74 record](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/history/seasons/1973/) supports that answer.

The European Cup wording through the 1992 final and Champions League wording from the 1993 final match UEFA's historical terminology. UEFA's [competition history](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/021b-0e9b869b0ed2-d14061c0ab62-1000--in-tune-with-a-new-football-world/) dates the distinctive Champions League presentation to 1992/93; later [UEFA season guidance](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0290-1bae124dbd1a-4a9fc08cd25c-1000--2024-25-champions-league-teams-format-dates-draws-final/) treats 1992/93 as the first edition under that name.

## The source-link defect

UEFA's season routes are not consistent across the reviewed years. The checked historical pages through the 2007 final use the starting year. The checked pages from the 2008 final use the ending year. The generator now contains an explicit mapping for the **65 reviewed finals**, so adding another final requires choosing its source rather than assuming a rule applies forever.

## Product assessment and remaining work

The factual answer keys and verified references are ready to support an approval decision for this scope. No unresolved paid answer contradiction was found. The following are product-quality considerations, not newly invented approval requirements:

- **Variety:** all 40 paid questions ask which club won a final in a given year. Their explanations repeat the answer and the same naming-history sentence. The store accurately describes a finite 40-question finals pack, but distinct match details and other historically grounded question forms would make the purchase more useful.
- **Difficulty:** the labels are provisional chronological estimates, not calibrated from player performance. No labels changed and no claim of measured beginner suitability is made.
- **Aliases:** the paid questions have no aliases. UEFA's shorter labels would fail the current answer engine if entered as text. The present UI offers typing only for `players`, so this does not break the paid multiple-choice flow. Add explicit, unambiguous aliases before extending typed play to these questions; avoid ambiguous labels such as “United.”
- **Coverage:** the other 95 bank questions and any future or edited news editions need their own factual/provenance review. Automated shape validation does not establish that those facts are correct.
- **Billing:** no Android/iOS purchase, cancellation, pending purchase or restore was performed by this content review. The existing commerce and release checks remain separate.

The repository's content note asks that every linked source and fact be verified before paid release. The release runbook asks for recorded actual editorial review; it does not create a human-only approval step. This document records completed independent verification within its stated scope and leaves the existing release decision and flag unchanged.
