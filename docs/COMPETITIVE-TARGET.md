The previous turn made verified progress: the Vercel deployment was repaired,
the visual preview published and six club-connection questions implemented.
That progress does not prove competitive quality or revenue readiness.

The target remains Football Quiz! Ultimate Trivia's overall experience. Its
Google Play listing advertises over 1,000 players/teams, hundreds of levels,
image recognition, typed answers, progression, hints, rewards, offline play,
ads and in-app purchases. These are listing claims, not independently audited
counts or evidence of revenue.

Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.football.quiz.trivia

Required completion evidence:

- At least 1,000 distinct, sourced, playable recognition/trivia items, with
  substantial player imagery rather than padding the total with repeated facts.
- A coherent player cartoon collection alongside photos, usable club/stadium
  art, complete provenance, and release review; five photos are insufficient.
- A professionally reviewed mobile design across home, level collection,
  question, results and shop; responsive motion and accessible interactions.
- Level/collection progression, replay and daily retention mechanics, with
  meaningful durable progress and no invented social statistics.
- Real configured iOS/Android products, verified purchasing/restoring/refund
  behavior, validated ad/consent integration if used, and accurate store prices.
- Launch measurement, privacy/support/store materials, physical-device testing,
  signed release builds and functioning distribution. No guarantee of revenue.
