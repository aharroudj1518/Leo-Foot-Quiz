# Stadium expansion

The visual stadium round grows from three to six grounds: Wembley, Allianz Arena, Maracanã, San Siro, Camp Nou and Stade de France. Three additional Commons photos are bundled offline with creators, licences, source links and file hashes recorded in assets/stadiums/manifest.json. They were visually inspected; commercial-release review is still pending.

Old Trafford and Estadio Azteca candidate photographs were rejected for prominent venue naming. Their metadata is retained as an audit record but they are not registered or bundled. The FNB Stadium candidate was skipped because its Wikipedia image was not available from Commons with accepted licence metadata. The importer resumes without replacing existing records.

Questions explicitly describe archive imagery rather than promising current stadium appearance. Text alternatives and source credits remain available. Home counts are derived from the visual bank.
